# Juss Beautiful Hair Private Operations Skill

## Who

This skill is for AI operators working in the private Juss Beautiful Hair control repository. The founder owns final vendor, product, pricing, publishing, outreach, purchasing, credential, and merge decisions.

## What

Use this skill for private hair operations: vendor intelligence, sample-first sourcing, quality evidence, internal catalog controls, care standards, product readiness, private admin changes, and exact-head verification.

Never expose supplier identities, negotiations, private pricing, customer information, credentials, or internal scoring in the public storefront or Untold Stories.

## When

Invoke it for vendor research, sourcing records, product evidence, internal admin work, catalog-governance changes, private workflows, or verification of the hair moat.

## Where

- `vendor-docs/` and other private operational records contain sourcing truth.
- `admin/` contains the private control application.
- `brand/BRAND_MOAT.md` defines the shared philosophy and product-separation boundary.
- `skills/juss-beautiful-hair-private/SKILL.md` defines AI behavior.
- Public customer-facing implementation belongs in `jussray/jussbeautifulhair-site`, not here.

## Why

The private repository protects the operational advantage behind the hair brand: supplier knowledge, quality proof, care discipline, and evidence-based expansion. Story improves meaning; it never weakens private sourcing controls.

## Exact-fix doctrine

The objective is the exact evidence-backed fix. Do not optimize for the smallest patch, fewest files, shortest response, or lowest effort when that leaves a known requirement unresolved. First define the full correctness, privacy, security, operational, verification, rollout, and rollback boundary. Then remove only unrelated work. Reversible stages are allowed; partial correctness presented as completion is not.

## How

Use the exact stack:

```text
/garyvee lindymode redteam l99 redteam ooda
```

1. Define the founder outcome and fastest truthful proof.
2. Prefer sample-first, reversible, low-inventory decisions.
3. Red Team the premise: determine whether a product, vendor, automation, or public claim should exist.
4. Map vendor provenance, landed cost, quality evidence, fulfillment, customer care, privacy, authority, and rollback.
5. Red Team the plan for counterfeit risk, weak samples, hidden costs, supplier lock-in, data leakage, and catalog contamination.
6. Define and implement the full evidence-backed fix, including every coupled change required for correctness and verification.
7. Re-observe through OODA and expand or contract the implementation when evidence changes.

## Product and data boundary

Juss Beautiful Hair retains bundles, closures, frontals, wigs, edge products, lace products, oils, hair suppliers, hair customers, hair checkout, and hair fulfillment.

Untold Stories apparel, caps, children's products, denim, accessories, footwear, story cards, vendors, customers, and checkout must not enter this repository's catalog or operational records.

Shared Story, Quality, Care, and Proof philosophy does not create shared inventory, suppliers, customers, checkout, credentials, or databases.

## Vendor decision contract

A vendor candidate may advance only when evidence covers:

- supplier identity and source provenance;
- product specifications and consistency;
- sample accessibility and sample results;
- landed cost, margin, lead time, and fulfillment path;
- customization and packaging capability;
- reliability, compliance, counterfeit, privacy, and concentration risk;
- customer-care implications and rollback options.

No AI may automatically contact, approve, purchase from, or publish a vendor. Founder approval is separate at each stage.

## Authority

Separate approval is required for:

- vendor outreach;
- sample orders or purchases;
- supplier approval;
- product activation;
- pricing or margin changes;
- customer communication;
- public publication;
- credentials, billing, database, deployment, merge, or destructive actions.

## Evidence

Required checks for material code changes are:

```text
cd admin
npm run verify:mcp
npm run check
npm run build
npm run verify:playwright
```

Evidence must be bound to the exact commit SHA. Skipped, stale, missing-log, `steps: null`, transferred, or truncated-critical results are not proof. Playwright must verify desktop, mobile, the existing hair catalog, signature products, and negative Untold-product leakage.

## Failure and rollback

Fail closed if supplier evidence is weak, private data may leak, the product boundary is uncertain, the exact head cannot be proven, or a check did not execute. Roll back through the exact commit, configuration, product state, or vendor-status change that introduced the risk. Never delete evidence to improve a score.

## Ten-year maintenance contract

- Version breaking changes to vendor, evidence, authority, catalog, or skill contracts.
- Keep private records exportable and provider-independent.
- Prefer additive data changes and documented deprecation windows.
- Review quarterly and after provider, commerce, runtime, privacy, or sourcing changes.
- Keep a tested separation between private operations and the public storefront.
- Preserve a controlled upgrade path; do not promise ten years without maintenance.

## Definition of done

Work is complete only when the exact final head passes every applicable check, the full required outcome is satisfied, private and public boundaries remain intact, founder-gated actions remain pending or explicitly approved, rollback is documented, and unresolved risks are zero or founder-accepted.
