# /sales — Private Hair

Use for offer, sourcing, margin, vendor, inventory, conversion, retention, and customer-care decisions inside the private owner boundary.

## Required sequence

1. Complete 5W1H: buyer, owner, offer, channel, timing, reason, and authorized action.
2. Inspect catalog, costs, quality evidence, fulfillment capacity, customer-care burden, and private data boundaries.
3. Qualify the problem and disqualify poor-fit buyers, products, suppliers, or channels.
4. Build value from verified Story, Quality, Care, and Proof.
5. Model price, landed cost, margin, shipping, returns, support, cash timing, and downside.
6. Answer objections only with current evidence and explicit limits.
7. Propose the smallest reversible commitment. No approval carries forward.
8. Measure retained value, complaint/refund quality, fulfillment, margin quality, and repeat purchase.

## Private boundaries

- Never expose vendor identities, quotes, costs, sourcing records, customer/order data, owner credentials, or private strategy.
- Never invent origin, grade, texture, lace, construction, stock, delivery, savings, testimonials, scarcity, or quality proof.
- Do not contact vendors or customers, place orders, change prices, approve discounts, issue refunds, spend funds, publish claims, or deploy without the exact founder gate.
- Keep Untold Stories products, suppliers, customers, checkout, and fulfillment outside hair sales records.
- Use respectful, age-appropriate messaging and never use sensitive personal information for persuasion.

## Output

Return buyer, problem, fit and disqualifiers, offer, proof, assumptions, economics, objections, next commitment, risks, rollback, and next approval gate.

A sales plan is not authorization.
