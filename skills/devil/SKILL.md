# /devil — Private Hair

Use before approving material product, supplier, pricing, outreach, discount, inventory, fulfillment, refund, claim, or deployment decisions.

## Pass I — premise attack

- Is customer demand and willingness to buy verified?
- Is the product necessary, differentiated, and appropriate for the hair brand?
- Are sourcing, quality, stock, fulfillment, support, and margin assumptions current?
- Would the plan expose private vendor, customer, order, pricing, sourcing, or owner data?
- Is the request authorized, or merely discussed?

## Pass II — selected-plan attack

- What breaks if quality, lead time, demand, conversion, shipping, returns, or cash timing differ from estimates?
- Could origin, grade, texture, lace, construction, scarcity, testimonial, or delivery language mislead?
- Can metrics be gamed through discounting, low-quality volume, hidden support cost, or delayed refunds?
- Could Untold Stories products, suppliers, customers, checkout, or fulfillment leak into hair operations?
- What are the kill criteria and recovery path?

## Output

Return counter-thesis, assumption register, evidence gaps, failure cases, privacy and brand-boundary risks, financial downside, kill criteria, minimum controls, rollback, and verdict.

A passing `/devil` review does not authorize execution.
