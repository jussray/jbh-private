# L99 Sales Authority — Private Hair

States: `observed → qualified → sampled → offer_draft → devil_reviewed → founder_approved → contacted → ordered → fulfilled → retained | refunded | stopped`.

No state authorizes the next. Separate founder gates govern vendor contact, sample and inventory purchase, supplier approval, product activation, price and discount changes, customer communication, publication, refunds, credentials, database changes, deployment, merge, and rollback.

Evidence binds project, product, offer version, supplier record, sample result, landed-cost assumptions, exact catalog state, owner, timestamp, and next gate. Public reports exclude vendor identities, costs, customer/order data, credentials, and private strategy.

Ambiguous or duplicate execution fails closed and is reconciled before retry.
