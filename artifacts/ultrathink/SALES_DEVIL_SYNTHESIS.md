# ULTRATHINK — Private Hair Sales + Devil

The private hair operation should maximize the probability that a right-fit customer receives a product whose story, specification, quality, care, fulfillment, and price agree.

`/sales` constructs qualification, offer, economics, proof, care, and retention. `/devil` attacks unsupported demand, misleading claims, supplier concentration, quality variance, cash timing, fulfillment failure, privacy leakage, and cross-brand contamination.

Operating chain:

`5W1H → evidence → qualification → premise attack → sample/quality proof → landed-cost model → L99 authority → plan attack → founder gate → smallest action → outcome evidence → OODA`

Disqualification is a success when it prevents a bad product, buyer mismatch, weak supplier, or unprofitable commitment. Private data remains private; aggregate lessons may transfer, source records may not.
