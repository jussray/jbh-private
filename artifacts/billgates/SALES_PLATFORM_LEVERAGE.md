# Bill Gates Artifact — Private Hair Sales Leverage

Turn private sourcing, sample, quality, care, fulfillment, margin, and retention evidence into a reusable operating platform without exposing vendor or customer records.

The reusable layer should version:

- product and offer definitions;
- sample and quality evidence;
- landed-cost assumptions;
- vendor performance categories;
- care/support requirements;
- objection and disqualifier patterns;
- fulfillment, refund, and retention outcomes;
- approval and rollback records.

Provider-specific systems remain replaceable behind adapters. Public storefronts receive only approved product facts and offer data; they do not receive private vendors, costs, sourcing negotiations, customer exports, owner credentials, or private strategy.

Ten-year leverage comes from portable evidence, disciplined product selection, repeatable quality, customer memory, and founder-controlled decisions—not dependence on one supplier, platform, or model.
