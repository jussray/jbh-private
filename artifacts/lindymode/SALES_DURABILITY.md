# Lindy Sales Durability — Private Hair

Prefer mechanisms that remain useful when vendors, platforms, models, and channels change:

- verified customer need;
- sample-first quality evidence;
- clear specifications and care guidance;
- current landed-cost and margin records;
- reliable fulfillment and support;
- repeat purchase earned through outcomes;
- portable private records with strict access boundaries.

Do not make one supplier, social platform, CRM, model, or checkout provider constitutional infrastructure. Preserve vendor abstraction, exportable evidence, reversible offers, and founder-controlled execution.

Durable advantage is Story + Quality + Care + Proof compounded through reliable operations—not aggressive scripts or temporary reach.
