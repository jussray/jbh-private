# Private Hair Sales Red Team

Premise risks: unverified demand, weak differentiation, unsupported origin or quality claims, supplier dependence, hidden support cost, discount-driven vanity revenue, and exposure of private vendor or customer data.

Plan risks: buying before sample proof, scaling before fulfillment proof, treating projected margin as realized cash, using urgency without inventory evidence, and transferring Untold Stories products or records into hair operations.

Controls: sample-first evidence, current landed-cost model, quality and care standards, explicit disqualifiers, private-data minimization, separate gates for vendor contact, purchasing, pricing, outreach, refund, publication, and deployment, plus kill criteria and rollback.

Verdict: decision support only; `/devil` reviews material commercial actions.
