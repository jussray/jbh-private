# Private Hair Sales Red Team

Premise risks: unverified demand, weak differentiation, unsupported origin/quality claims, supplier dependence, hidden support cost, discount-driven vanity revenue, and accidental exposure of private vendor or customer data.

Plan risks: buying before sample proof, scaling before fulfillment proof, treating projected margin as realized cash, using urgency or scarcity without inventory evidence, and transferring Untold Stories products or records into the hair operation.

Controls: sample-first evidence, current landed-cost model, quality and care standard, explicit disqualifiers, private-data minimization, separate gates for vendor contact/purchase/pricing/outreach/refund/deployment, kill criteria, and rollback.

Verdict: `/sales` remains decision support only; `/devil` must review material commercial actions.
