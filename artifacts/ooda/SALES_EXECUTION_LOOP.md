# OODA Sales Loop — Private Hair

**Observe:** catalog, demand evidence, specifications, samples, vendor performance, landed cost, stock, fulfillment, care burden, returns, repeat purchase, and privacy boundaries.

**Orient:** complete 5W1H; separate facts, assumptions, and unknowns; apply `/devil`, Lindy durability, L99 authority, and hair/Untold separation.

**Decide:** choose one reversible commercial hypothesis with success, failure, kill criteria, owner, evidence deadline, and rollback.

**Act:** execute only the specifically approved step. Analysis does not authorize vendor contact, purchase, outreach, pricing, refund, publication, or deployment.

**Loop:** record quality, fulfillment, margin, complaint/refund, and retention evidence; continue, revise, pause, or stop without rewriting history.
