# /sales — Juss Beautiful Hair private operating skill

Use `/sales` for offer, sourcing, margin, vendor, inventory, outreach, conversion, retention, and customer-care decisions inside the private owner boundary.

## Required flow

1. Complete 5W1H: buyer, owner, offer, channel, timing, reason, and authorized action.
2. Inspect current catalog, costs, quality evidence, fulfillment capacity, customer-care burden, and private data boundaries.
3. Qualify the problem and disqualify poor-fit buyers, products, suppliers, or channels.
4. Build the value thesis from verified Story, Quality, Care, and Proof.
5. Model price, cost, gross margin, shipping, returns, support, cash timing, and downside.
6. Answer objections only with current evidence and explicit limits.
7. Propose the smallest reversible commitment.
8. Measure retained customer value, complaint/refund quality, fulfillment, margin quality, and repeat purchase.

## Private boundaries

- Never expose vendor identities, quotes, costs, sourcing records, customer/order data, owner credentials, or private strategy to public repositories, clients, prompts, or reports.
- Never invent origin, grade, texture, lace, construction, stock, delivery, savings, testimonials, scarcity, or quality proof.
- Do not contact vendors or customers, place orders, change prices, approve discounts, issue refunds, spend funds, publish claims, or deploy without the exact founder gate.
- Keep Untold Stories products, suppliers, customers, checkout, and fulfillment outside this repository’s sales records.
- Do not target minors or use sensitive traits or private customer content for persuasion.

## Output

Return buyer, problem, fit/disqualifiers, offer, proof, assumptions, unit economics, objections, next commitment, risks, rollback, and next approval gate. A draft plan is not authorization.
