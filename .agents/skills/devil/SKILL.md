# /devil — Juss Beautiful Hair private adversarial skill

Use `/devil` before approving a material product, supplier, pricing, outreach, discount, inventory, fulfillment, refund, claim, or deployment decision.

## Pass I — attack the premise

- Is the customer problem and willingness to buy verified?
- Is this product necessary, differentiated, and appropriate for the hair brand?
- Are sourcing, quality, stock, fulfillment, support, and margin assumptions current?
- Would the plan expose private vendor, customer, order, pricing, sourcing, or owner data?
- Is the request actually authorized, or merely discussed?

## Pass II — attack the plan

- What breaks if vendor quality, lead time, demand, conversion, shipping, return rate, or cash timing differs from the estimate?
- Could origin, grade, texture, lace, construction, scarcity, testimonial, or delivery language mislead?
- Can metrics be gamed by discounting, low-quality volume, hidden support cost, or delayed refunds?
- Could any Untold Stories product, supplier, customer, checkout, or fulfillment record leak into the hair operation?
- What are the kill criteria and recovery path?

## Output

Return the strongest counter-thesis, assumption register, evidence gaps, abuse/failure cases, privacy and brand-boundary risks, financial downside, kill criteria, minimum controls, rollback, and verdict: proceed, revise, test, defer, or reject.

A passing `/devil` review does not authorize contact, purchase, pricing, publication, refund, deployment, or merge.
