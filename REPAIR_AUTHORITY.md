# JBH Private Commerce Repair Authority

This repository owns the private paid-order control path. It follows the portfolio necessary-fix default with a hard separation between repairing infrastructure/integration and exercising commercial/customer/vendor authority.

## Required binding

Every consequential repair must bind to the exact repository/base/head, verified JBH Shopify store fingerprint, Cloudflare Worker/service identity, private database identity, endpoint/resource identity, failure fingerprint, evidence-only proof cookies, before state, rollback, and post-action readback.

Changing any load-bearing identity expires predecessor proof and repair authority.

## Source / CI repair authority

Trusted repository operators may repair source, tests, workflows, non-secret configuration, migrations, validation, documentation, and exact-head evidence on the existing carrier. They may rerun failed GitHub jobs when diagnostically valid and may merge only when the repository's current exact-candidate policy independently authorizes it.

## Cloudflare private-runtime repair authority

A trusted provider executor may restore the already-approved private Worker/custom-host topology or deploy an already-authorized exact release when:

- canonical account/service/hostname fingerprints are re-verified;
- the action is bounded and reversible;
- no credential creation/rotation/exposure, new spend, ownership transfer, or destructive deletion is required;
- the previous provider state is retained for rollback;
- `/health` and immutable release identity are read back after mutation;
- applicable real-path proof passes.

A mismatching provider service identity must fail closed rather than be silently adopted.

## Database repair authority

A trusted data-plane executor may apply an already-reviewed, exact-subject, forward-only non-destructive migration when preflight passes, rollback/forward compensation is defined, and live schema is read back afterward.

Destructive data changes, customer/order deletion, privilege expansion, credential changes, and ambiguous historical migration rewrites remain explicit founder gates.

## Shopify webhook/config repair authority

A trusted provider executor may create, repair, or remove the exact already-approved `orders/paid` webhook subscription when all of the following are independently verified:

- canonical JBH store fingerprint;
- exact approved webhook topic and HTTPS endpoint;
- endpoint runtime/health identity;
- no broader app scope or credential change;
- operation is idempotent/reversible;
- post-write Shopify readback confirms exactly the intended subscription.

Webhook registration is integration authority only. It is not authority to create charges, issue refunds, change prices, alter orders, dispatch vendors, fulfill orders, contact customers, or move funds.

## Hard commercial gates

Explicit founder/business authority remains required for:

- charging/refunding or otherwise initiating money movement;
- price/discount/inventory/shipping/return/checkout/payment changes;
- customer/order mutation beyond deterministic processing of already-paid signed Shopify events under existing policy;
- vendor/supplier dispatch or purchase commitments;
- fulfillment decisions with external consequence;
- app-scope expansion, secrets operations, spending/billing, contracts, ownership transfer, destructive operations, domains/DNS ownership changes, or external customer/vendor communication outside approved automation.

## Witnesses

GitHub/local tests may trigger source repair. Cloudflare may trigger runtime repair. Shopify provider readback may trigger webhook/config repair. Playwright/browser verifies applicable user/admin paths but cannot authorize commercial action. Linear/Asana, Notion/Drive/docs, Slack, and Gmail may repair their own record/evidence state only unless a separate explicit automation delegates a specific action.

Fingerprints and proof cookies are evidence-only. For consequential repairs report REALITY, WITNESSES, FIX, PROOF, RISK, ROLLBACK, NEXT GATE.
