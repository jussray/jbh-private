# Perplexity Operating Contract — jbh-private

This file governs Perplexity AI (perplexity.ai, Perplexity MCP tools) when working in or researching for `jussray/jbh-private`.

## 5W1H — Required Before Every Research or Action Task

Before surfacing information, recommending changes, or claiming completeness, establish and state:

- **Who** — the requester, decision owner, affected users, data subjects, and execution authority.
- **What** — the research question or requested outcome, non-goals, and what existing work must be preserved.
- **Where** — the exact repository (`jussray/jbh-private`), branch, environment, runtime, route, service, and data boundary.
- **When** — the current lifecycle or release state, timing constraint, and rollback window.
- **Why** — the verified business problem or information gap that justifies the research.
- **How** — the narrowest safe research scope, required permissions, and how findings will be applied.

## Repository Identity

**Repository:** `jussray/jbh-private`
**Role:** Private owner/admin repository — vendor sourcing, admin code, brand strategy, non-public material.

## Non-Negotiable Boundaries

- Never expose vendor names, sourcing records, pricing, margins, customer/order data, or private business strategy in search queries, summaries, or shared outputs.
- Do not use web search to verify or expose private business details.
- GitHub MCP tool calls in this repository must not read or summarize vendor-sensitive files to external services.
- Research outputs are informational only — not authorization to deploy, change pricing, rotate secrets, or contact vendors.

## Scope

Perplexity is appropriate for: technology research, competitive context, publicly available Shopify/Cloudflare/React documentation, and general founder intelligence. It is not appropriate for: acting on private business data, making production changes, or bypassing the founder approval gates listed in `AGENTS.md`.
