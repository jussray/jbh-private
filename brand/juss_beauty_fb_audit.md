## 3. Likely gaps in the Juss Beauty page

Based on patterns in newer independent beauty supply pages — and adjusting if your page is already further along — these are the most common gaps to verify against the checklist above:

1. **No Facebook Shop or incomplete catalog.** Without a Shop tab, you lose product tagging in posts and Reels, which is the single highest-leverage conversion mechanic on the platform.
2. **Inconsistent visual identity.** Mixed photo styles, varied lighting, and rotating fonts/colors make a page look like a personal profile rather than a shop. All five benchmarks lock to a tight brand kit.
3. **Low Reels share of content.** Reels get distributed beyond your follower base; static photos mostly reach existing followers. If Reels are <30% of your posts, you're under-indexing.
4. **No tutorial content.** Tutorials drive saves, shares, and follows — and they're the type of content beauty supply audiences explicitly search for.
5. **CTA buttons not optimized.** Many shop pages leave the default "Message" button without adding "Shop Now," a website link, or a WhatsApp/SMS contact.
6. **Slow or no comment replies.** Meta surfaces a "Very responsive" badge when reply rate is high; it doubles as a trust signal for first-time visitors.
7. **No cross-linked Instagram/TikTok.** A Facebook page in isolation under-performs; cross-posting from Instagram Reels and TikTok is how all five benchmarks fill their content calendar without extra production work.
8. **No branded hashtag or community hub.** Bevel built #BEENBEVEL; Private Label built a 40K-member Facebook Group. You don't need 40K — even 100 members in a "Juss Beauty Insiders" group becomes a sales channel.

## 4. Prioritized 30-day setup roadmap

Tasks ranked by **impact ÷ effort**. Do the P0 items this week — they unlock everything else.

### P0 — Foundation (Days 1–3, must-have)

1. **Convert the page to a Business / Shop category.** In Settings → Page Info, set category to "Beauty Supply Store" (or "Cosmetics Store"). This unlocks Shop and product-tagging eligibility.
2. **Complete the About section.** Address, hours, phone, website, email, short bio with keywords ("beauty supply," "hair," your neighborhood). Pages with complete info rank higher in Facebook search.
3. **Upload a professional profile photo and cover photo.** Profile = your logo on a solid background (square, min 320×320). Cover = a hero product flat-lay or storefront photo (1640×856). Keep text inside the safe area.
4. **Add the "Shop Now" CTA button.** Point it to your website's product page or to your Facebook Shop once it's live.
5. **Verify your domain** in [Meta Business Suite](https://business.facebook.com/) → Brand Safety → Domains. Required for full Shop and ads functionality.

### P1 — Shop & product tagging (Days 4–10)

6. **Open [Commerce Manager](https://business.facebook.com/commerce_manager) and create your shop.** Use the Meta-hosted checkout for the simplest path, or connect Shopify/WooCommerce if you already sell online.
7. **Build your product catalog.** For each SKU: title (with brand + variant), description (≥100 chars, include benefits and ingredients), price, condition, ≥3 photos (hero + 2 lifestyle/detail), inventory, shipping weight. See [Meta's catalog quality guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2086567618225367).
8. **Create at least 4 collections.** Examples: "Wigs & Units," "Bundles & Closures," "Hair Care," "Tools & Accessories." Collections drive discovery inside the Shop tab.
9. **Set shipping zones and return policy** inside Commerce Manager → Settings.
10. **Tag products in your last 10 posts.** Use [Facebook's product-tagging flow](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1655062744733202) — tagged posts get a shopping bag icon and show up in Shop discovery.

### P2 — Discovery & branding (Days 11–20)

11. **Lock a brand kit:** one font (e.g., Montserrat or Poppins), two brand colors, one accent color. Apply to every graphic via a free Canva template.
12. **Reshoot your top 10 products** with consistent lighting (window light + white poster board works) and a single background color. Replace catalog photos as you go.
13. **Publish 6 Reels in the first 2 weeks**, themed:
    - 2x install/application tutorials
    - 2x product demos with on-screen pricing
    - 1x "new arrivals" haul
    - 1x customer transformation (UGC or in-store)
    Use vertical 9:16, hook in first 3 seconds, on-screen captions, tag products.
14. **Connect Instagram to the page** in Meta Business Suite. Cross-post automatically so one piece of content fuels both platforms.
15. **Pin a "Start Here" post** at the top of the page: hours, location, top 3 products, link to shop, link to Instagram.
16. **Turn on Reviews/Recommendations.** Ask 10 recent in-store customers via text to leave one.

### P3 — Engagement engine (Days 21–30)

17. **Set Messenger auto-reply** with a 4-button FAQ menu: "See products," "Store hours," "Place an order," "Speak to someone."
18. **Commit to a 24-hour reply SLA** on comments and DMs. Aim for the "Very responsive to messages" badge (90% response rate, <15 min during open hours).
19. **Launch a branded hashtag** (e.g., #JussBeautyGlowUp). Use it in every post and ask customers to tag.
20. **Create a "Juss Beauty Insiders" Facebook Group** linked to the page. Seed it with first-look launches, member-only discounts, and styling tips. Even 50 active members beats 5,000 silent page followers.
21. **Schedule one giveaway** in week 4: follow + tag 2 friends + share to enter. Prize = a $50–$100 bundle of bestsellers.
22. **Set a posting cadence** of 5 posts/week minimum: Mon (new arrival), Wed (tutorial Reel), Thu (UGC repost), Fri (sale/restock), Sat (behind-the-scenes or founder content).
23. **Add a weekly Live** — Friday 7 PM is the sweet spot for beauty. Show new stock, answer questions, run a flash discount code.
