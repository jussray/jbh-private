# Juss Beautiful Hair — Brand + Shopify Launch Plan
**Lawless And Flawless · Premium Hair & Beauty Supply**

A bootstrap-friendly (under $100 this month), dropship-based launch plan to turn your Facebook page into a real brand with a Shopify store. Built for your actual situation: 16 followers today, no inventory, no studio.

## Part 3 — Product strategy (the dropship mix)

Since you're dropshipping, you want a focused starter catalog of **15–25 products** across 4 collections. Don't list 200 items on day one — it makes the store look like a generic site. Curation = brand.

### Recommended starter collections

| Collection | Number of products | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Bundles & Wefts** | 6–8 | Body wave, straight, deep wave, kinky curly — in 14"/18"/22"/26" |
| **Lace Wigs & Units** | 4–6 | HD lace frontals, ready-to-wear glueless wigs, U-parts |
| **Closures & Frontals** | 3–5 | 4×4, 5×5, 13×4 HD lace |
| **Beauty Essentials** | 4–6 | Edge control, hair oil, wig caps, melting bands, lace tint spray |

### Dropship vendors to evaluate (in priority order)

1. **[Dropship Beauty](https://www.dropshipbeauty.com)** — built specifically for hair extension dropshipping, integrates with Shopify, includes pre-built website templates. Best fit for your model.
2. **[Private Label Extensions Dropship (Dropship Bundles)](https://www.privatelabelextensions.com/pages/dropshipping-hair-extensions)** — same company that runs the 40K-member Facebook group. Monthly subscription, no minimum orders, US warehouse.
3. **[Spocket](https://www.spocket.co)** — US/EU based dropshipping suppliers for the beauty essentials (oils, edges, accessories). 2–5 day shipping. Direct Shopify integration.
4. **[Blanka](https://www.blanka.co)** — private-label beauty (your logo on the bottle). Use this later for your own signature edge control or hair oil.
5. **[Zendrop](https://www.zendrop.com/beauty-products/)** — fallback for trending beauty accessories.

**Start with one vendor.** Adding more comes after your first 10 sales, not before.

### Pricing rule of thumb for dropship hair
Cost from vendor × 2.5 to 3 = your retail price. Example: $40 wholesale bundle → $99–$120 retail. Anything tighter than 2x and shipping fees will eat your margin.

## Part 4 — Shopify setup (step by step, in order)

### Day 1 — Account + theme (45 minutes)

1. Go to **[shopify.com/free-trial](https://www.shopify.com/free-trial)** → start the 3-day free trial → after day 3, take the $1/month × 3 months offer on the Basic plan ([details](https://www.shopify.com/pricing)).
2. **Store name:** Juss Beautiful Hair. **Industry:** Beauty. **Are you selling already?** Just starting.
3. In Settings → Domains, **buy `jussbeautifulhair.com`** (~$14/yr through Shopify so it's auto-connected). If it's taken, try `shopjussbeautifulhair.com` or `jussbeautyhair.com`.
4. **Pick a free theme** from [themes.shopify.com/collections/free-themes](https://themes.shopify.com/collections/free-themes). Best free options for beauty/hair:
   - **Dawn** (clean, fast, beginner-friendly) ← recommended for you
   - **Refresh** (more editorial, great for hero photography)
   - **Studio** (luxe, gallery feel)
5. In Theme settings, set:
   - **Primary color:** `#2D1A4A`
   - **Accent color:** `#B89BD9`
   - **Background:** `#FAF7FC`
   - **Headings font:** Playfair Display
   - **Body font:** Montserrat
6. Upload your logo (use the `jbh_profile.png` file) and a favicon (same image, 32×32).

### Day 2 — Catalog (1–2 hours)

7. Sign up for **Dropship Beauty** (or Dropship Bundles). Install their Shopify app from the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com).
8. Import **15–25 products** across the 4 collections above. Don't keep their stock photos as-is — they're shared with every other dropshipper. Use Canva to add your purple brand frame, "Juss Beautiful Hair" watermark, and price tag.
9. For each product, **rewrite the title and description in your brand voice**. Example:
   - ❌ Vendor default: "22 inch body wave Brazilian virgin hair bundle"
   - ✅ Your version: "Lawless Body Wave Bundle — 22" · Soft, bouncy, dye-friendly. Ships in 2–3 days from the US."
10. Add at least one **bestseller bundle** (3-bundle deal at a slight discount) — this becomes your hero product on the homepage.

### Day 3 — Pages, policies, payments (1 hour)

11. **Pages to create** (Online Store → Pages):
    - About — your story (2 short paragraphs). Why "Lawless And Flawless," who you serve, what makes you different.
    - Shipping & Returns — Shopify has a free generator at Settings → Policies.
    - FAQ — 5 questions: shipping time, returns, how to choose length, can I dye it, do you offer install services.
    - Contact — form + your email + DM links.
12. **Payments:** turn on **Shopify Payments** (Settings → Payments) so customers can use credit card, Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay. Add **PayPal** as a secondary option.
13. **Shipping:** Settings → Shipping. For dropship, set flat-rate domestic ($8–$12) and free over $150. Your vendor handles fulfillment; you just need the customer-facing rate.

### Day 4 — Connect Facebook & Instagram (30 minutes)

14. Sales channels → add **Facebook & Instagram** channel.
15. Connect your Juss Beautiful Hair Facebook page and Instagram (if you have one — if not, create `@jussbeautifulhair` IG today).
16. Shopify auto-syncs your catalog to **Meta Commerce Manager**. Wait 24–48 hrs for Meta approval.
17. Once approved, the **Shop tab** appears on your Facebook page, and you can **tag products in posts and Reels**.
18. **Verify your domain** in Meta Business Suite → Brand Safety → Domains. Required for full Shop functionality.

### Day 5 — Soft launch (1 hour)

19. Add a **homepage banner**: hero image with "Lawless And Flawless" + "Shop Hair" + "Shop Beauty" buttons.
20. Add a **featured products** row with 4 bestsellers.
21. Set up an **email capture popup** ("Get 10% off your first order") — free with Shopify Email.
22. **Place a test order yourself** to make sure checkout, email confirmation, and vendor handoff all work.
23. Go live.

## Part 7 — What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't run paid ads until you have 30+ followers and a working store. Ads to a sparse page convert at near-zero.
- ❌ Don't list 100+ products on day one. Curate to 15–25 — looks more premium and is manageable.
- ❌ Don't use vendor-default product photos without adding your branded frame. Every dropshipper has the same photos; the watermark and color treatment is what makes it *yours*.
- ❌ Don't promise 1-day shipping you can't deliver. Set realistic 3–5 business day expectations.
- ❌ Don't skip the test order. Broken checkouts kill launches.
